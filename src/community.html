Where this is saved. Off The Block is a static site with no server behind it, so everything you post here is stored in your own browser, on your own device. That means your notes are private to you and nothing is uploaded anywhere — but it also means other visitors won't see them yet. Use "Export as CSV" if you want to send your feedback on.

Comments here are personal experience, not medical advice, and they are not checked by anyone qualified. What worked for one swimmer may not suit you — body size, training load, age, and health history all change the answer. Talk to a doctor or a registered sports dietitian before changing anything significant, and never start a supplement on the strength of a comment.
